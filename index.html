01 — The gap

When you fall, two systems are waiting. Neither was built for you.

An injury. A burnout. A failed exam. A career break. The moment you're knocked off track, only two kinds of help exist — and both miss the person who is still capable, just off course.

The performance system

Expects you to keep climbing. Has no vocabulary for a pause.

The person in between

Still capable. Knocked off track. No structured way back in.

The crisis system

Steps in once things have fully broken down. Not before.

That gap is where self-doubt comes from. Not the setback itself — being left to interpret it alone, with no plan and no one checking in on the way back.

So people either push through silently until they break again, or quietly disengage. Both get read as a lack of ability. Neither is.

COStrong builds the system that's missing: structured support for the return itself.
